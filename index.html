Upside and Downside of NHL 27

On the upside, NHL 27 breathes fresh energy into the franchise with the long-awaited return of Connected Franchise mode, allowing up to 32 players to run custom leagues together online, alongside updated broadcast packages, new playbooks, and arena audio overhauls that elevate the game's atmosphere. However, the downside lies in its execution; despite these structural additions, critical reactions have been decidedly mixed due to persistent gameplay stagnation, controversial implementation of AI-generated commentary voices, and strikingly inaccurate player face models that draw substantial criticism from fans.
